# HR Security & Workforce Lifecycle Standard

**Owner:** Security / People
**Effective:** 26 August 2026
**Review:** at least annually and after material legal, organisational or risk change

## Scope
Applies to employees, contractors and third parties with access to Raeburn systems, source code, customer information, AI services or confidential business information.

## Pre-engagement screening
Risk-based background verification is required before privileged or sensitive access is granted, subject to applicable law, proportionality, privacy and contractual requirements. Screening scope must be documented and appropriate to the role and jurisdiction.

## Agreements and confidentiality
Personnel must accept written employment/engagement terms before access is granted. Terms must include confidentiality, information-security, privacy, acceptable-use and intellectual-property obligations appropriate to the role. Separate NDA terms are used where required.

## Roles and responsibilities
Security, privacy and acceptable-use responsibilities form part of role expectations. Privileged roles are expressly identified and access is limited to approved duties.

## Technology acceptable use
Organisation-owned and authorised technology may only be used for lawful, approved business purposes. Unapproved software, credential sharing, bypassing security controls and moving confidential data to unapproved services are prohibited.

## Clean desk and clear screen
Unattended workspaces must not expose confidential information. Devices must be locked when unattended. Printed confidential material must be secured or securely destroyed.

## Remote and home working
Remote work must use managed/approved devices where required, encrypted connections, screen locking, MFA where supported, secure local environments and approved storage/collaboration services. Confidential material must not be exposed to unauthorised household/public access.

## Joiner, mover, leaver
Access is provisioned only after approval. Role changes trigger access review. On termination or contract end, access is revoked promptly, credentials/tokens are disabled, organisation-owned assets are returned, and residual local/company data is removed or secured.

## Security awareness
All relevant personnel must receive security/privacy awareness on joining and periodic updates covering phishing/social engineering, password/MFA hygiene, data handling, incident reporting, remote working, acceptable use and AI-specific risks. Role-specific training is required for privileged, engineering, security, privacy and AI roles.

## AI competency and acceptable use
Personnel using or administering AI services must receive training appropriate to their role covering data classification, prompt injection, model limitations, human oversight, hallucination, privacy, copyright/IP, prohibited data, approved providers, output review and escalation. Use of unapproved AI services for confidential/customer data is prohibited.

## Evidence
Security/People retain applicable screening records, signed agreements, access approvals, training completion evidence, asset return records and termination/access-revocation records in controlled systems.
