# Provider Assurance Evidence Requirements

For CSA AI-CAIQ controls that depend on managed cloud/datacentre suppliers, Security must retain evidence for the actual production providers before final attestation.

Required evidence includes, where applicable: current SOC/ISO/CSA or equivalent assurance; encryption-at-rest/in-transit and key-management documentation; physical-security perimeters and access controls; access-log retention; CCTV/surveillance coverage; datacentre personnel training; cable protection; environmental monitoring; utility resilience/testing; geographic/physical resilience; secure media handling/destruction; and incident/continuity commitments.

Evidence must be dated, linked to the applicable provider/service and reviewed at least annually or after a material supplier/service change. Public provider documentation may support a control where authoritative and current; restricted audit reports may be retained under NDA rather than published.
