# AI Human Oversight Standard

**Owner:** Security / Product
**Effective:** 26 August 2026

Raeburn AI systems are decision-support tools unless a separately approved use case states otherwise. Material legal, financial, employment, safety, compliance, customer-contract or other high-impact decisions require human review and approval.

Human reviewers must be able to inspect relevant inputs, assumptions, source context, generated output, confidence/limitations and material risks before acting. AI output must not silently override explicit user, customer, legal, security or policy constraints.

Where an automated action is permitted, the action scope, allowed tools, data permissions, rollback/stop mechanism and escalation route must be defined in advance. Operators may pause, reject, correct or escalate AI output.

The Proposal Generator produces advisory proposal content only. Generated proposals, pricing, ROI estimates, risks and recommendations must be reviewed by an authorised consultant before external reliance or contractual use.

Evidence of oversight may include approval records, review logs, change history, exception records and incident/quality review outcomes.
